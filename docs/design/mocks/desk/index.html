The Desk

An agent workspace designed for one owner who runs five persistent agents, mostly from a phone. Chat, work and agents come first. Everything else sits behind Control.

  1. Colour means somethingThe interface is monochrome. Amber means a person is needed. Green and red are outcomes. Only agent sigils carry other colour.
  2. Decide from anywhereApprovals and questions are cards you answer where you are. A tray in every conversation lists what needs you in other agents.
  3. Say what you want doneOne composer, three intents: ask now, delegate in the background, or repeat on a schedule.
  4. Agents are a crewEach has a sigil, a live line and a state ring. Agents can hand work to each other, and the hand-off shows in the thread.
